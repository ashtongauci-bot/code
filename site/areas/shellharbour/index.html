Dilapidation reports in Shellharbour

We're based in Oak Flats, so Shellharbour is our home ground. Pre- and post-construction reports across the whole city.

Shellharbour has two kinds of building site. In the established suburbs like Warilla, Barrack Heights, Oak Flats and Albion Park, it's knockdown-rebuilds, granny flats and duplexes on lots with neighbours a metre away. In the growth areas of Shell Cove, Calderwood, Tullimbar and Flinders, it's new houses going up beside homes that were finished only a year or two earlier.

Both situations lead to the same question when a crack appears next door: was it there before? A dilapidation report answers it.

Common jobs in Shellharbour

  • Knockdown-rebuilds and duplexes in the older suburbs, where neighbouring brick veneer homes often already show settlement cracking
  • Pools, where excavation and compaction sit very close to the boundary. See pool dilapidation reports
  • New builds in estates, where a newer neighbouring home may be mid-way through its own settlement
  • Commercial and mixed-use work around Shellharbour City Centre and the marina precinct

Why a local engineer helps

Being nearby means we can inspect at short notice when a start date moves, and come back quickly for the post-construction inspection. It also means we know the housing stock: what's typical for a 1970s brick veneer in Warilla and what isn't.

Suburbs we cover

Every suburb in the City of Shellharbour, including Shell Cove, Shellharbour Village, Oak Flats, Mount Warrigal, Lake Illawarra, Warilla, Barrack Heights, Blackbutt, Flinders, Albion Park, Albion Park Rail, Calderwood, Tullimbar and Dunmore.

Common questions

Do new estates like Calderwood or Shell Cove need dilapidation reports?

When a new house or pool goes in next to an occupied home, a pre-construction report on the neighbour is good protection for the builder, even if the consent doesn't require one. In growing estates many lots are built at different times, so occupied homes regularly end up beside active sites.
