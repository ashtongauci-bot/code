Dilapidation reports in Campbelltown and Macarthur

Pre- and post-construction inspections across Campbelltown, Camden and Wollondilly, just over the hill from the Illawarra.

Macarthur is one of Sydney's main growth corridors. New estates in Oran Park, Gregory Hills and Gledswood Hills sit beside established suburbs, and infill duplexes are replacing older homes in Campbelltown, Ingleburn and Minto.

Common jobs in Macarthur

  • Civil and subdivision works beside existing homes, with weeks of earthmoving and compaction
  • Road upgrades, where a run of properties along the corridor needs recording
  • New homes in growing estates built next to houses that are only a few years old
  • Infill duplexes and townhouses in the established suburbs around Campbelltown

Campbelltown City Council, Camden Council and Wollondilly Shire Council each set their own dilapidation conditions. Send us yours and we'll match the report to them.

Suburbs we cover

Campbelltown, Leumeah, Bradbury, Ambarvale, Rosemeadow, Ingleburn, Minto, Glenfield, Macquarie Fields, Camden, Narellan, Oran Park, Gregory Hills, Gledswood Hills, Harrington Park, Mount Annan, Spring Farm, Appin and Picton.

Common questions

Can you inspect along a road or civil works corridor?

Yes. We inspect each property along the corridor and issue one consistent set of reports, which makes the post-construction comparison straightforward.

Is Macarthur close to you?

Yes. Macarthur is a short drive from the Illawarra over Appin Road, so it's part of our regular area.

Need a dilapidation report?

Send the address and what's being built. We'll come back with a fixed fee and an inspection date.
