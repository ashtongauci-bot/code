Sydney councils routinely make dilapidation reports a condition of consent where demolition, excavation or basement works sit close to a boundary. On tight inner-city lots, the neighbouring houses are often within a metre of the works, or share a wall with them.

Our Sydney work is backed by experience on large commercial and residential structures, as well as house-scale inspections. Reports are inspected and signed by a Chartered Professional Engineer.

Sydney jobs we're suited to

  • Basement excavation beside occupied homes and strata buildings, where shoring and dewatering can affect neighbours
  • Terraces and semis with shared party walls in the Inner West, Eastern Suburbs and St George
  • Multi-property sites where several neighbours, a strata building and council assets all need recording
  • Sutherland Shire and southern Sydney, which are a short drive from our Illawarra base

Strata buildings

When a neighbouring building is a strata scheme, we can inspect common property such as car parks, stairs, facades and lobbies, along with individual lots whose owners agree to access. We coordinate access through the strata manager.
