Eastern Suburbs projects are often ambitious: new basements under existing homes, full rebuilds on tight coastal lots, and apartment buildings replacing older blocks. Neighbours are close, properties are valuable, and owners pay attention to every crack.

Why careful records matter here

  • Sandstone excavation for basements and pools, with rock sawing and hammering close to neighbouring footings
  • Sandy coastal ground around Bondi, Maroubra and parts of Randwick, where older buildings may already show settlement
  • Terraces in Paddington, Woollahra and Surry Hills with shared party walls and decorative detail
  • Older apartment blocks with many owners, where common property and individual units need inspecting

Waverley, Woollahra and Randwick councils regularly require dilapidation reports for adjoining properties where excavation is involved. We'll make sure the report suits your conditions and is ready before works start.
