The Shire sits between our Illawarra base and the rest of Sydney, so it's an area we cover often. Building work here ranges from apartment blocks near Cronulla and Miranda to duplexes in Caringbah and Gymea and new homes on steep waterfront blocks in Como, Oyster Bay and Grays Point.

What makes Shire sites different

  • Sandstone. Much of the Shire sits on Hawkesbury sandstone close to the surface. Pools, basements and level building pads often mean rock sawing and hammering, which sends vibration into neighbouring homes.
  • Sloping waterfront blocks along the Georges River and Port Hacking, with stepped footings, retaining walls and boatsheds that need recording.
  • Sandy ground near Cronulla, where older homes often already show settlement cracking.
  • Duplex and dual occupancy builds on standard lots, with neighbours very close to the works.

Sutherland Shire Council commonly sets dilapidation conditions where excavation is close to a boundary. Send us the condition wording with your quote request and we'll make sure the report covers it.
