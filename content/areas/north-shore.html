The North Shore combines steep sandstone sites, high-value homes and growing centres like Chatswood, St Leonards and Crows Nest. Excavation into rock on sloping blocks is common, and neighbours are often uphill or downhill of the works.

What we see on the North Shore

  • Steep sites in Mosman, Cremorne, Lane Cove and Northbridge, where excavation can affect retaining walls and the homes above and below
  • Sandstone excavation for basements, pools and garages close to boundaries
  • Heritage homes in conservation areas across Ku-ring-gai and Willoughby, with original plaster, chimneys and detail worth recording carefully
  • Apartment and mixed-use projects around Chatswood, St Leonards and Crows Nest beside older homes and strata buildings

North Sydney, Willoughby, Lane Cove, Mosman and Ku-ring-gai councils each set their own dilapidation conditions. Send us yours and we'll match the report to it.
