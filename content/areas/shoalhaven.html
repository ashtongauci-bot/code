The Shoalhaven is one of the fastest-growing parts of regional NSW. Around Nowra and Bomaderry that means new subdivisions, road upgrades and commercial building. Along the Bay and Basin and the southern coast it means knockdown-rebuilds and holiday homes replaced with larger permanent houses.

Wherever heavy works sit near occupied homes, a dilapidation report records their condition before work starts, so any damage claim can be settled on evidence.

Common jobs in the Shoalhaven

  • Subdivision and civil works, where earthmoving and compaction run alongside existing homes for weeks
  • Road and drainage upgrades, with a row of properties along the corridor to record
  • Coastal knockdown-rebuilds in Huskisson, Vincentia, Culburra Beach and Mollymook, often on sandy ground where existing settlement cracking is common
  • Commercial and multi-unit projects in Nowra and Ulladulla

Suburbs we cover

Nowra, Bomaderry, North Nowra, Worrigee, Berry, Shoalhaven Heads, Culburra Beach, Callala Bay, Huskisson, Vincentia, Sanctuary Point, St Georges Basin, Sussex Inlet, Ulladulla, Mollymook and Milton.
