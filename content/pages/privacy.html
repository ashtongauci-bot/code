Privacy

When you send a quote request we receive your name, contact details, the site address and anything you attach. We use this only to prepare your quote, arrange inspections and deliver your report.

Form submissions are processed by our website host, Netlify, and delivered to us by email. We don't sell or share your details, and we don't add you to marketing lists.

Inspection reports contain photographs of private property. They are provided to the person who commissioned the report, and to the owner of each inspected property on request.
