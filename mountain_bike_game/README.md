# Mountain Bike Madness

A 2D side-scrolling mountain bike game that runs entirely in the browser —
no install, no build step, no dependencies.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (double-click it, or drag it into a
browser window).

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ↑ (Up) | Jump |
| ↓ (Down) | Tuck — crouch low for extra speed on downhills (also boosts your next jump) |
| ← (Left) | Rotate backward in the air (backflips) |
| → (Right) | Rotate forward in the air (frontflips) |

- Hills are procedurally generated and get bigger the further you ride.
- Land full flips to score big points — multiple flips in one jump score
  even more.
- Match the slope when you land: touch down too crooked and you crash.
  Press any key after a crash to restart.
- Score also ticks up with distance travelled, and the HUD shows your speed.
